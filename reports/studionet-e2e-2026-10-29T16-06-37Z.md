## How to read this report

- **Refusal checks** prove the contract rejects bad input. A refusal passes only when the network decided the call failed (or could not run it) AND the contract state read back afterwards is byte-for-byte unchanged.
- **Structure** checks are facts about the echo graph that follow from addresses alone (for example two hosts of one GitHub account are one origin). They must hold.
- **Forbidden verdicts** must never be issued (for example a clean bill for a known myth, or while a source is unreadable). Breaking one fails the run.
- **Hoped verdicts** depend on live pages and on the validators' language model. They are reported, never enforced.
- Every excerpt recorded on chain was also searched for in a page copy fetched by this script. A miss is a warning: the validators may have been served a different copy.
